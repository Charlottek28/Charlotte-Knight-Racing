# Rounds 9 & 10 of the British Enduro Championship 🏁

A weekend in Devon and another step in the right direction. The speed is getting there, but I'm still giving away far too much time with mistakes and spending a bit too much time on the floor! 😂

I really enjoyed the Cross Test this weekend. Faster and less technical, but still requiring you to be smart with your lines and carry good speed through the corners. The track had been ripped and, surprisingly, the first few laps had loads of grip, which made it great fun.

I struggled more with the XC Test and finding a consistent flow. One particular test didn't go to plan when I somehow managed to get myself stuck in a tree 🤦‍♀️ costing around 3 minutes! Along with a few other costly mistakes, it massively hurt the overall.

The frustrating part is that when I put a lap together, the times were competitive, so the speed is definitely there — I just need to put more of the good laps together and keep myself off the floor! 😂

It's okay though, we're building. Pace is improving and there's plenty to take away from the weekend. 💪🏁

Result: P3 on both days.

Onwards!
